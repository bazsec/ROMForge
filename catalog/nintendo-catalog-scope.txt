ROMForge Nintendo North American catalogs
Prepared 2026-10-11

These are working catalogs of original-era licensed North American releases,
not Nintendo-certified definitive inventories or emulator compatibility lists.
Source URLs appear on every row. Do not automatically delete unmatched ROMs or
covers from these lists until the system's inventory has been reconciled.

Additional systems in this batch:
Game Boy: 507 entries (499 main-list games, 2 separately listed compilations,
  6 utility cartridges including Game Boy Camera and the InfoGenius titles).
Game Boy Color: 444 entries, including dual-mode cartridges. Dual-mode status
  is recorded in Notes. These are not duplicated in the original Game Boy list
  just because the same cartridge can also run on that system.
Game Boy Advance: 949 entries from the main games baseline. At the user's
  request, the 39 supplemental compilation and game/video cartridges were
  removed. Do not add later repackaged multi-game cartridges. Original releases
  already in the baseline, such as Namco Museum and Final Fantasy I & II: Dawn
  of Souls, remain included. GBA Video-only cartridges and e-Reader cards remain
  excluded. This is a working catalog, not a certified exhaustive inventory.
Nintendo 64: 296 entries. The Japan-only 64DD and later aftermarket releases
  are outside the scope.
GameCube: 555 entries from the main games table, including full-game promotional
  compilations in that table. The separate bonus/demo discs table is excluded.

General exclusions: unlicensed/homebrew releases, prototypes, cancelled North
American releases, post-lifespan aftermarket releases, Japan/PAL-only releases,
and packaging-only variants. Regional reissues are not exhaustively itemized.
"Game" in Release type follows the main source games table and can include a
compilation. "Compilation" identifies a separately tabulated compilation, not
an exhaustive classification of all multi-game products.

Dates preserve the source precision (year, month, or full date). Historical
sources sometimes disagree on shipment versus street dates. The catalogs are
not independently verified against every cartridge or publisher record.

Title preserves the catalog's North American name. Filename stem removes
filesystem-reserved characters, changes ':' to ' -', changes '/' to ' + ', and
moves a leading 'The' to ', The'. These are proposed names, not names verified
against the user's files. Each system has unique case-insensitive filename
stems. ROM and cover matching has not been performed. No user ROMs, icons, or
running apps were modified by creating these catalogs.
